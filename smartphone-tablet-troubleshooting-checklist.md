# Smartphone & tablet troubleshooting checklist (iPhone, iPad & Android)

A plain-English, step-by-step guide for small businesses and individuals when an
iPhone, iPad or Android phone/tablet is slow, running low on storage, crashing,
or having battery or connectivity problems. Work through the steps in order —
each is safe and reversible. If you reach the end without a fix, the notes you
have collected will make professional help much faster and cheaper.

## Before you change anything

- [ ] Restart the device — this clears most transient faults and is always the first step.
- [ ] Note exactly what changed recently — a new app, a system update, a full storage, a dropped device.
- [ ] Back up anything irreplaceable (photos, notes, contacts) before deeper fixes.
- [ ] Write down any exact error message, word for word.

## Slow or sluggish device (all platforms)

- [ ] Close apps you are not using (swipe them away from the app switcher).
- [ ] Check free storage. Aim for at least 10% free:
  - iPhone/iPad: Settings → General → iPhone/iPad Storage.
  - Android: Settings → Storage.
- [ ] Clear space by removing unused apps, old downloads and large videos.
- [ ] Install pending operating-system and app updates, then restart.
- [ ] Reduce motion/animations if the device is older (Settings → Accessibility).

## Battery drains too fast

- [ ] Check battery usage to see which app is consuming the most (Settings → Battery).
- [ ] Reduce screen brightness and shorten the auto-lock time.
- [ ] Turn off Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and location services when not needed.
- [ ] Check battery health (iPhone/iPad: Settings → Battery → Battery Health; Android varies by maker).
- [ ] If the device gets hot, remove the case and stop heavy apps until it cools.

## Apps crash or will not open

- [ ] Force-close the app and reopen it.
- [ ] Update the app from the App Store or Google Play.
- [ ] Restart the device.
- [ ] If still crashing, delete the app and reinstall it (check you know your login details first).

## Wi-Fi or mobile data problems

- [ ] Toggle Wi-Fi off and on; forget and rejoin the network (re-enter the password).
- [ ] Restart the router and the device.
- [ ] Check whether other devices on the same network are affected.
- [ ] For mobile data: confirm you have not exceeded your plan allowance and that data is switched on.
- [ ] If abroad, check roaming is enabled for the SIM you are using.

## Low storage warnings

- [ ] Review what is using space (Settings → Storage) and delete the largest unused items.
- [ ] Offload large photos/videos to a computer or cloud, then delete local copies.
- [ ] Clear app caches where offered (Android: Settings → Apps → Storage → Clear cache).
- [ ] Delete old messages with large attachments.

## When to stop and get help

- [ ] Stop if a fix feels risky or if irreplaceable data is at stake.
- [ ] Summarise what changed, what you tried, and the exact error message.

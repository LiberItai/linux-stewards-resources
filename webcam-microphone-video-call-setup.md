# Webcam & microphone setup for video calls

Plain-English steps for getting your camera and microphone working on Zoom, Teams,
Google Meet or similar — before you miss another call. This is a free guide from
[Linux Stewards](https://www.linuxstewards.com); if you would rather have it done
for you, we provide remote IT help worldwide.

## Start here (in order)

1. **Check the physical basics.** A built-in camera often has a privacy shutter or a
   switch — make sure it is open. Check any USB webcam is plugged in, and that a
   headset or microphone is connected to the right socket.
2. **Restart the app.** Fully quit the meeting app (not just close the window) and
   reopen it, then rejoin the call.
3. **Check which device the app is using.** In the app's settings, pick the correct
   camera and microphone from the list — a laptop may list several and default to the
   wrong one.

## Camera not working

- Check that only one app is using the camera at a time; close any other app that may
  have grabbed it.
- On Windows, check **Settings → Privacy & security → Camera** and allow the app.
- On a Mac, check **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera** and allow the app.
- In the browser (for web-based calls), click the padlock in the address bar and allow
  camera and microphone for that site.
- Update the webcam driver, or try a different USB port for an external camera.

## Microphone not working / people cannot hear you

- Check the app is not muted, and that the correct microphone is selected in its settings.
- Speak while watching the input level meter in the app's audio settings; if it does not
  move, the wrong device is selected or the mic is blocked.
- Check **Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone** (Windows) or **Privacy & Security →
  Microphone** (Mac) and allow the app.
- If you use a headset, check its inline mute switch or button.
- Use the app's built-in "test microphone" to confirm before joining.

## Audio or video quality problems

- Move closer to the router if on Wi-Fi, or plug in a network cable for important calls.
- Close other apps that are uploading or streaming, and pause large downloads.
- Point a light toward your face and avoid a bright window directly behind you.
- Use headphones to stop echo and feedback; turn the speaker volume down slightly.

## When to call for help

- The camera or microphone still does not appear after the checks above.
- You need a better camera, microphone or lighting for regular customer-facing calls.
- You want a set-and-forget setup so every call "just works" without fiddling.

## Keep it healthy

- Update your meeting app and the operating system regularly.
- Keep the camera lens clean with a soft, dry cloth.
- Keep the webcam and microphone drivers updated.
- Test camera and microphone in the app settings the day before an important call.

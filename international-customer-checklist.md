# Working with Linux Stewards internationally

Linux Stewards is based in the United Kingdom and provides remote help worldwide
where appropriate. This page sets out, in plain English, what to agree and expect
before work starts, so there are no surprises.

## Before we start — confirm together

1. **Scope** — exactly what is and is not included in the job.
2. **Price** — quoted in GBP and agreed before any work starts.
3. **Currency & payment** — the currency you pay in and the payment method.
   Any currency conversion or transfer fees are the customer's responsibility,
   not ours.
4. **Delivery** — how the finished result reaches you (file, access, link or
   screen-share).
5. **Time zone** — we operate on UK time. We agree realistic response and
   availability together before committing.
6. **Third-party costs** — domains, hosting, software licences, subscriptions,
   paid APIs, stock assets and app-store fees are separate and paid by you.
7. **Language** — English (Phase 1).

## What we do not claim

- A local office or local presence in your country.
- Local telephone support in your country.
- Tax or legal advice about your jurisdiction.

If any point above is unclear, ask before work starts. Small jobs are genuinely
welcome — entry fixes start from £12.

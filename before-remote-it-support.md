# Before remote IT support: a practical checklist

Use this checklist to explain a Windows, Mac, Linux, website or everyday IT problem clearly. It helps keep the first conversation focused on the smallest useful fix.

## Gather the useful details

- **What are you using?** Note the device, operating system and affected application or website.
- **What were you trying to do?** Describe the expected result and what happened instead.
- **What does the error say?** Copy its wording or take a screenshot. Hide passwords, account numbers, private messages and other people's information.
- **When did it last work?** Mention a recent update or change if you know one; it is fine to say you do not know.
- **How widespread is it?** Is one file, one device or everyone affected?
- **What have you tried?** A short list avoids repeating work.
- **What matters most?** Say whether there is a deadline, important data or a limit on the time or budget available.

## Before anyone changes the device

Save your work. Tell the technician whether important files are backed up and whether you have checked that a backup can be opened. Agree what may be changed and how the session will end.

Do not put passwords, recovery codes or private keys in an enquiry form. Describe an account-access problem without sharing the secret.

## A message you can use

> I need help with [device/application]. I expected [result], but [problem] happened. It last worked [when/unknown]. The error says [text]. I have tried [steps]. My main priority is [priority]. Please confirm the scope before work begins.
